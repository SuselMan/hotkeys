## Voicing notes

- "Nine ninety-nine" reads cleaner than "$9.99" — neural TTS tends to choke
  on the dollar sign or pronounce it as "dollar nine point nine nine".
- "kekkeys dot online" instead of "kekkeys.online" for the same reason; TTS
  often reads the literal dot otherwise inconsistently.
- Pronounce **kekkeys** as a single beat — `kek-keez`. If the model splits
  it, add a phonetic hint or train a custom voice on the word.
- Tone: confident, dry, technical. Not announcer voice. Not guru voice.
  Closer to a friend explaining a tool than a launch trailer.
- "Macropad" is the category anchor — say it clearly in the hook, that
  word does the heaviest lifting for what kekkeys *is*.

## Localisation roadmap (post v1.0)

- ru, es, de, ja audio tracks dropped over the same video using the v1.0
  visuals. Each translation pass is 130–200 words, similar 60–90s pacing.
- Localised ru should keep Russian sentences short — a literal translation
  of the en script runs ~15% longer in characters and breaks the timing.
